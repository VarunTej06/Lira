## 1. What we collect, and why

| Data | Why we need it |

## 2. Consent
We ask for your consent at sign-up, in plain words, before we collect your data, and keep a record of it. Marketing email and marketing cookies are **separate choices, off until you switch them on**. You can withdraw any consent at any time in settings, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing does not undo what we did before; some features may stop working if the data they need is withdrawn.

## 3. Where your data is kept
In Microsoft Azure data centres **in India**. Files are kept in encrypted storage and the database is encrypted. Data moves between your browser and us over encrypted connections.

## 6. Your rights
You can, at any time, in settings or by writing to us:
- ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we shared it with;
- **correct or complete** it;
- **erase** it, which also deletes your account;
- **download** your data;
- **withdraw consent**;
- name another person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act;
- **complain** to us, and, if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 7. Cookies
We use cookies that are needed to sign you in and keep the service secure; these cannot be switched off. **Analytics and marketing cookies are off until you accept them** in the banner; you can change your choice at any time from the link in the footer.

## 8. Security
Access is limited by role and by organisation, passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, and changes are logged. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
